# Toy Front Loader Teardown and Rebuild

This procedure describes how to take apart and rebuild a small plastic toy vehicle (a front loader,
tractor, or similar construction toy) that is held together with a handful of screws.

## Tools

- A small screwdriver sized for the toy's screws (manual or battery powered).

## Procedure

1. Using the screwdriver, remove every screw holding the cab, chassis, axles and wheels together,
   and separate the toy into its individual loose parts.
2. Set all the removed parts and screws down on the table next to the disassembled toy.
3. Reassemble the toy: reattach the axles and wheels to the chassis, reattach the cab, and drive
   each screw back in with the screwdriver until the toy is fully rebuilt.
4. Confirm the rebuilt toy holds together as a single unit before finishing.

## Requirements

- The toy must be fully taken apart into its separate loose parts before reassembly begins.
- The toy must be fully reassembled into one piece by the end of the procedure.
